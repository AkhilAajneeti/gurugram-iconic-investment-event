Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who should attend the meet?
Anyone evaluating South Gurgaon — whether for a plot to build on, an independent floor, a branded residence or commercial space. All four Signature Group projects are presented together, so you can compare formats and pricing in one sitting.
Is there an entry fee?
No. Entry is free, but registration is mandatory — only registered visitors are permitted inside. Register on this page and our team will call you to confirm your slot.
What will I see at the event?
Masterplans and layouts for all four projects, available inventory, pricing and payment plans, connectivity and infrastructure details for the Sohna Road corridor, and the chance to speak directly with the project teams.
Can I bring family or business partners?
Yes. Please indicate the number of attendees in the registration form so we can allot seats for your group in your chosen slot.
Is the pricing shown final?
No. ₹1 Lakh – ₹1.35 Lakh per sq. yd. is indicative starting pricing. Final rates, inventory and payment plans are confirmed by the developer at the time of booking.
What if I cannot attend on 20 September?
Register anyway and select “No, I won’t be able to attend”. Our team will share the project details with you and arrange a separate discussion or site visit.
